# About Me & Tau Ideas

Hi! I'm **Marcos Javier Martínez Ramos**, and **Tau Ideas** is the digital space where I centralize my passion for technology, software development, and personal projects with a real-world impact. 

I have been deeply involved in the chess world for over 35 years in the A Coruña area (Spain)—as a competitive club player (Club de Ajedrez Alexandre Bóveda) and as an instructor in schools and organizations like Xadrez das Mariñas. Throughout my journey, I've learned that chess and software development share the exact same essence: strategy, logic, and creative problem-solving.

That is why I created **Tau Ideas**, a hub designed to host my own digital platforms and applications. 

If you want to learn more about my projects, collaborate, or just chat about tech and chess, feel free to reach out at [hello@tauideas.tech](mailto:hello@tauideas.tech).
